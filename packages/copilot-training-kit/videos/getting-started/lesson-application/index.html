    About this video

    The third unit's steps, narrated: have Copilot build a small tip calculator, ask what it does, and ask which lines work out the tip. Pause where the video says, do the step on your computer, then play on.

    This video walks through the unit's three written steps. If your screen doesn't match, the written steps say what to do.

    1. Have Copilot build a small tip calculator, and open its folder in the list on the left.
    2. Ask Copilot what the tip calculator does, and what each file is for.
    3. Ask which lines work out the tip, then open the file and check the lines.
