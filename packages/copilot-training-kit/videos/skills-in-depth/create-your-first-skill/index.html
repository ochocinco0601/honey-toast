    About this video

    The unit's steps, narrated: ask for a meeting summary with every instruction typed out, have Copilot save those instructions as a skill, and get the same summary by asking in one line. Pause where the video says, do the step on your computer, then play on.

    This video walks through the unit's four written steps. If your screen doesn't match, the written steps say what to do.

    1. Have Copilot create practice meeting notes in a new folder, then open the folder and trust it.
    2. Ask for a meeting summary, with every instruction typed out.
    3. Have Copilot save those instructions as a skill, and open its file.
    4. Start a new chat, and ask for the summary in one line.
