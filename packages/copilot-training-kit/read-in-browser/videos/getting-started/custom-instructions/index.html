    About this video

    The fourth unit's steps, narrated: ask for a summary, have Copilot save custom instructions for summaries, ask again, and remove them. Pause where the video says, do the step on your computer, then play on.

    This video walks through the unit's four written steps. If your screen doesn't match, the written steps say what to do.

    1. Ask Copilot to summarize the meeting notes.
    2. Have Copilot save custom instructions for summaries, and open the file.
    3. Start a new chat, ask for the summary again, and compare.
    4. Have Copilot delete the custom instructions.
