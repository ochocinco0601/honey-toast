# Practice files

Made-up files for practising, with no real people, customers or systems in them. Use them whenever
you are not sure your own files are allowed, until your organization tells you.

- `notes/`: three weekly meeting notes, with decisions, action items and open questions.
- `tickets.csv`: a ticket export, twenty-five rows.
- `changes/`: three change requests awaiting approval, and a checklist to review them against.

To use them, copy the whole `sample-files` folder to your Documents folder, then add that copy to
VS Code: menu File, then Add Folder to Workspace. Work on the copy, so this training stays as it is.
