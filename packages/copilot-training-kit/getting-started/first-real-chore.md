# Your first real chore this week

The last unit of the path, on your own work. Best after
[Lesson: your own files](lesson-your-files.md).

Before you close this page, write two lines at the top of `my-requests.md` in your own folder: the
chore you will do, and the day. Put fifteen minutes in your calendar for that day.

On the day:

1. **Open the recipe for that kind of chore first,** from the list below, and read its "Before you
   start" or "Before you export". For a fresh export, remove the columns the data rule rules out
   before the assistant sees it.
2. **Write the request yourself** in `my-requests.md` in your folder, with the five parts from
   [Lesson: your own files](lesson-your-files.md#6-see-how-your-request-was-built), and save it.
   Copy the line below into the chat box, paste your request after it, and press Enter:

   <!-- tutor -->
   > Before you do anything, tell me which of these is missing or unclear in the request below, and do not carry it out: which folder, what to read, what to make and where, what not to do, show your sources. The request:

3. **Send it.** Change your request in `my-requests.md` using what it said, save it, copy it into
   the chat box on its own, and press Enter. Allow only commands that name the folders and files
   your request named.
4. **Check two items** against the files: one you knew in advance, and one you would act on.
5. **Compare** your request with the recipe's: if it has a part yours lacked, add it next time.

The recipe for each kind of chore:

- **Tickets, exports or spreadsheets:** this week's export; compare with
  [Analyze an exported spreadsheet](how-to/analyze-an-export.md). A good result is a table you
  checked against the export yourself.
- **Decisions, approvals or planning:** the next change or document that lands on you; compare
  with [Review a change request](how-to/review-a-change-for-go-or-no-go.md) or
  [Find what is missing from documentation](how-to/find-what-is-missing-from-documentation.md).
  A good result points at the passage for every answer.
- **An application you support:** one question you would normally ask its owner, asked as in
  [Lesson: an application, step 5](lesson-application.md#5-of-7-explore-by-the-question-you-actually-have).
  A good result is one you checked in the file it names.
- **A chore no recipe covers:** [Write your own request](how-to/write-your-own-request.md).

Check: a week from now, your folder holds the file the assistant wrote, with the items you checked
marked, and `my-requests.md` with the request you wrote. That is your record.

## You have finished the path

You can now point the assistant at a folder of your own work, write a request it can carry out and
you can check, allow only what you asked for, and check the result against the files. Three habits
go with you:

- **A request has five parts:** which folder, what to read, what to make and where, what not to
  do, and show your sources.
- **Know one answer in advance,** and see that it comes back.
- **Check what you would act on,** in the file it names.
