# Knowing whether it worked

Attendees liking the session is the weakest evidence there is. Training that people enjoy and
never use afterward passes that test. The evidence that matters is behavior a week later.

## During the session

- Setup check: how many replied within the 60 seconds. Count from chat.
- Practice: at the folder step, ask for a thumbs-up in chat when the folder appears. Count.

## One week later, one question

Send: "Since the session, have you had the assistant do one chore on your own files? Reply with
one line saying what, or 'not yet' and what stopped you."

The "what stopped you" replies are the next session's content.

## What to change next run

Keep a short list per run, outside this folder. Three kinds of entry are worth recording: what
was spoken but not shown; which question had no answer in the room; which step took longest.
