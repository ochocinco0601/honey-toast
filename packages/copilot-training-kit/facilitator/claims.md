# Claims register

Every sentence the trainings state about what the subject does, what a screen shows, or what the
organization requires, with where it came from. Following the steps does not test whether they are
true; checking each claim against its source does. Not built into pages.

This register covers the written pages.

Class is one of: observed (seen on screen or in the source), documented (from the subject's own
documentation), told (someone who does the work said so), unverified, blank (left for the
organization).
