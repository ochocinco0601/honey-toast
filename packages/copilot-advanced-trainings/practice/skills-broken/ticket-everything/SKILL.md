# Ticket summary

**Folder:** work only in the folder the user names.

**Read:** the ticket export.

**Make, and where:** a file named `ticket-summary.md` in that folder, with these headings:
`## In numbers` (total, open, closed, and counts by category), `## Oldest open` (three tickets),
`## Keeps coming back` (repeated problems with ticket numbers).

**Do not:** change the files you read, or copy names, email addresses or account numbers into the
summary.

**Show your sources:** every count and observation names its ticket numbers.
