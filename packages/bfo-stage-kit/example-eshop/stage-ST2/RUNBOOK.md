## 1. Alert and expectation

**What fired.** M13 Orders past the acceptance promise, by state: above the operators' threshold for longer than their window.

**The expectation it guards.** A case that enters this stage leaves it within the promise (proposed: 2 minutes from entering Submitted, read from the timing of L03, L04 and L06: a 1-minute grace period and up to 30 seconds to the next poll (OrderProcessor/appsettings.json:15-16; OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodManagerService.cs:17, 51), then 10 seconds before StockConfirmed and 10 before Paid (Ordering.API/Application/Commands/SetStockConfirmedOrderStatusCommandHandler.cs:22; Ordering.API/Application/Commands/SetPaidOrderStatusCommandHandler.cs:22). The stage is entered when the record is created, so a case's age in it is now minus "OrderDate" (Ordering.Domain/AggregatesModel/OrderAggregate/Order.cs:58)), in one of these ways:

- Step 5: The order is cancelled because an item is out of stock: (proposed) Cancelled, with Description naming the products without stock
- Step 6: Payment is taken for the order: (proposed) Paid, with the order total taken once from the buyer's payment method
- Step 7: The order is cancelled because payment failed: (proposed) Cancelled, with no payment taken
- Step 8: The order is cancelled at the customer's request: (proposed) Cancelled before any payment is taken

**Why a person is paged.** A case left by L04, L05, L06, L07 is stranded: nothing in the application moves it again.

Check §7's triggers at every step, not only at the end.

## 2. What is at stake

- **Order id ("Id")**: The only key that joins the order across services and to the customer
- **Order total (sum of Units x UnitPrice)**: Money the customer pays; value blocked while the order is stuck
- **Items and units (ordering."orderItems")**: What must be in stock and shipped
- **Buyer (ordering.buyers "IdentityGuid")**: Who is waiting and who is told
- **Delivery address**: Where the goods must arrive
- **Payment method (card number masked to the last four digits)**: What is charged
- **Irreversible:** Payment is taken for the order (L06) cannot be undone.
- Step 3: customer, counted in orders held in Submitted past the grace period. Every poll publishes again for orders still Submitted (OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodManagerService.cs:54-61).
- Step 4: customer, counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation. A lost or failed message is not sent again; nothing polls AwaitingValidation (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180; OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodOrdersRepository.cs:27).
- Step 4: customer, counted in orders confirmed with an item the catalogue does not hold. The check skips a product it cannot find, so the order is confirmed (Catalog.API/IntegrationEvents/EventHandling/OrderStatusChangedToAwaitingValidationIntegrationEventHandler.cs:17-24).
- Step 5: customer, counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation after their stock was rejected. Same mechanism as step 4 (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Step 6: customer, counted in orders stranded in StockConfirmed, not paid. A lost payment request or result is not sent again (PaymentProcessor/IntegrationEvents/EventHandling/OrderStatusChangedToStockConfirmedIntegrationEventHandler.cs:32; EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Step 6: financial, counted in order totals not collected for orders stranded in StockConfirmed. Value blocked: the order total (Ordering.Domain/AggregatesModel/OrderAggregate/Order.cs:185) is not taken while the order waits.
- Step 7: customer, counted in orders stranded in StockConfirmed after a failed payment. Same mechanism as step 6 (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Step 8: customer, counted in cancel requests that fail, so the order goes ahead. The customer gets an error and must try again (Ordering.API/Apis/OrdersApi.cs:41-46). A cancel that lands while payment is in flight leaves the order Cancelled and the payment result ignored (Ordering.Domain/AggregatesModel/OrderAggregate/Order.cs:121); with a real gateway that would be money taken for a cancelled order.

**What not to do.**

- Do not set "OrderStatus" straight to 'StockConfirmed', 'Paid' or 'Cancelled' by SQL: the events are raised only inside the setters, so no outbox row is written and nothing after it happens (payment, stock reduction, notifications)
- Do not set an outbox row's "State" back to 0 to have it sent: nothing reads unsent rows after the request that wrote them
- Do not look for failed messages to replay: a consumer acknowledges a message it failed on, and no dead-letter queue exists
- Do not restart order-processor to move orders held in AwaitingValidation or StockConfirmed: the poll selects only Submitted
- Do not restart the postgres or eventbus container apps as a first move: no data volume is declared for either, so find out where their data lives first
