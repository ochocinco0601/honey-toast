    About this explainer

    An example of the explainer page: replace this line with what the piece is and who it is for.

    This example has three scenes: an opening that names the subject and what the viewer will be able to do, a before and after shown in one frame, and a close on the first thing to do. A real piece puts its own summary here, or the job aid's text when there is one.

    Using the player

    Space

    C

    The progress bar reports the scene: it holds while a scene plays and steps at the boundary. Sound is optional: turned off, the captions carry the narration, and the piece stops when each scene has finished drawing until you press play.
