Each row is one flow identified. Each dot is one of the flow’s milestones toward supported in production. Identified, Mapped, Measures defined, Runbooks written: filled once drafted for your review (a runbook is drafted, never rehearsed). Alerts defined: filled once the review of the proposed alerts is recorded. Instrumented, Dashboards built, Team prepared: done by other teams, so these dots never fill here. The count under Instrumented is read from the code at design time, not from the running system. A draft is not complete, correct or accepted. The line between two filled dots is filled. Click any dot to see what is there to review at that milestone.
