## Alert M13: Orders past the acceptance promise, by state

### What fired

**M13 Orders past the acceptance promise, by state** is above the operators' threshold for longer than their window ([ALERT.md](ALERT.md)).

**The expectation it guards:** an order still in Submitted, AwaitingValidation or StockConfirmed past the promise has not been accepted when the shopper expects, and the moves out of AwaitingValidation and StockConfirmed strand it with nothing to move it again (lifecycle L04-L07, recovery stranded)

**Its cases:** those held in the stage past the bound (the age bound in [ALERT.md](ALERT.md)). A case not past the bound is not one of M13's.

**Window:** from when the oldest of its cases entered the stage, to now. The alert fires only once a case has waited past the bound, so its cause can be as old as that: never read a test over only the time since the count began rising.

Check the triggers to escalate or declare an incident ([S8](#s8-escalate-or-declare)) at every step, not only at the end.

### Opening checks

Run T1, T2 and T3 ([S4](#s4-every-test-once)) over the window above: an open incident or known error, a planned window or change, and what changed since the window opened. Then T4: is the count falling?

### Contain first

No hold is needed: the cases M13 counts are still in the stage, and nothing more is done to them while they wait. Take none of the actions under **What not to do** ([S6](#s6-restore): R8, R9, R10, R11, R12).

### S2. What is at stake

- **Order id ("Id")**: The only key that joins the order across services and to the customer
- **Order total (sum of Units x UnitPrice)**: Money the customer pays; value blocked while the order is stuck
- **Items and units (ordering."orderItems")**: What must be in stock and shipped
- **Buyer (ordering.buyers "IdentityGuid")**: Who is waiting and who is told
- **Delivery address**: Where the goods must arrive
- **Payment method (card number masked to the last four digits)**: What is charged
- **Irreversible:** Payment is taken for the order (L06) cannot be undone.
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is released for stock checking when the grace period ends” fails: counted in orders held in Submitted past the grace period. Every poll publishes again for orders still Submitted (OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodManagerService.cs:54-61).
- Customer experience and trust, if “Stock is confirmed for every item of the order” fails: counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation. A lost or failed message is not sent again; nothing polls AwaitingValidation (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180; OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodOrdersRepository.cs:27).
- Customer experience and trust, if “Stock is confirmed for every item of the order” fails: counted in orders confirmed with an item the catalogue does not hold. The check skips a product it cannot find, so the order is confirmed (Catalog.API/IntegrationEvents/EventHandling/OrderStatusChangedToAwaitingValidationIntegrationEventHandler.cs:17-24).
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is cancelled because an item is out of stock” fails: counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation after their stock was rejected. Same mechanism as step 2.2 (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Customer experience and trust, if “Payment is taken for the order” fails: counted in orders stranded in StockConfirmed, not paid. A lost payment request or result is not sent again (PaymentProcessor/IntegrationEvents/EventHandling/OrderStatusChangedToStockConfirmedIntegrationEventHandler.cs:32; EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Money directly at stake, if “Payment is taken for the order” fails: counted in order totals not collected for orders stranded in StockConfirmed. Value blocked: the order total (Ordering.Domain/AggregatesModel/OrderAggregate/Order.cs:185) is not taken while the order waits.
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is cancelled because payment failed” fails: counted in orders stranded in StockConfirmed after a failed payment. Same mechanism as the expected outcome “Payment is taken for the order” (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is cancelled at the customer's request” fails: counted in cancel requests that fail, so the order goes ahead. The customer gets an error and must try again (Ordering.API/Apis/OrdersApi.cs:41-46). A cancel that lands while payment is in flight leaves the order Cancelled and the payment result ignored (Ordering.Domain/AggregatesModel/OrderAggregate/Order.cs:121); with a real gateway that would be money taken for a cancelled order.
- **Declare an incident** when the impact threshold in the severity definition is crossed: an operator value, linked here.
