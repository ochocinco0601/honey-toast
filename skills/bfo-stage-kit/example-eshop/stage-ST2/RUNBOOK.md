## Alert M13: Orders past the acceptance time limit, by state

### What fired

**M13 Orders past the acceptance time limit, by state** is above the operators' threshold for longer than their window ([ALERT.md](ALERT.md)).

**The expectation it guards:** an order still in Submitted, AwaitingValidation or StockConfirmed past the time limit has not been accepted when the shopper expects, and the moves out of AwaitingValidation and StockConfirmed strand it with nothing to move it again (lifecycle L04-L07, recovery stranded).

This measure is not emitted yet; deployed as written, the alert fires on No data until it is.

**Its cases:** those held in the stage past the time limit (the time limit in [ALERT.md](ALERT.md)). A case not past the time limit is not one of M13's.

**Window:** from when the oldest of its cases entered the stage, to now. The alert fires only once a case has waited past the time limit, so its cause can be as old as that: never read a test over only the time since the count began rising.

Check the triggers to escalate or declare an incident ([S8](#s8-escalate-or-declare)) at every step, not only at the end.

### Opening checks

Run T1, T2 and T3 ([S4](#s4-every-test-once)) over the window above: an open incident or known error, a planned window or change, and what changed since the window opened. Then T4: is the count falling?

### Contain first

No hold is needed: the cases M13 counts are still in the stage, and nothing more is done to them while they wait. Take none of the actions under **What not to do** ([S6](#s6-restore): RM11, RM12, RM8, RM9, RM10).

## Shared section: Accept the order

### S1. The stage on one page

**Configuration described:** An online store selling outdoor clothing and equipment to signed-in customers through a web front end (and a mobile app), with orders accepted, stock-checked, paid and shipped by back-end services joined by a message broker. Configuration described: the Aspire AppHost (eShop.AppHost/Program.cs:1-104) as deployed to Azure Container Apps (eShop.AppHost/Program.cs:6), with UseFoundry=true so the chat and embedding models are present (eShop.AppHost/Program.cs:80-85; Ollama is hard-coded off at :87-91); Postgres, RabbitMQ and Redis run as the application's own containers (eShop.AppHost/Program.cs:8-19). proposed: no source says which configuration runs in production; this is the fullest the repository defines. In this configuration the services run outside Development, so /health is not mapped (eShop.ServiceDefaults/Extensions.cs:115-125) and logs default to Information (Ordering.API/appsettings.json:4).

Flow **Fulfilling a customer's order**, one case: One order. Stage 2 of 4, **Accept the order**. [STRUCTURE.md](STRUCTURE.md) draws the same steps and [MEASURES.md](MEASURES.md) measures them.

**Expected of the stage.** A case that enters it leaves within the time limit (proposed: 2 minutes from entering Submitted, read from the timing of L03, L04 and L06: a 1-minute grace period and up to 30 seconds to the next poll (OrderProcessor/appsettings.json:15-16; OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodManagerService.cs:17, 51), then 10 seconds before StockConfirmed and 10 before Paid (Ordering.API/Application/Commands/SetStockConfirmedOrderStatusCommandHandler.cs:22; Ordering.API/Application/Commands/SetPaidOrderStatusCommandHandler.cs:22). - Expected outcome: Payment is taken for the order: (proposed) Paid, with the order total taken once from the buyer's payment method
- Other outcome: The order is cancelled because payment failed: (proposed) Cancelled, with no payment taken
- Other outcome: The order is cancelled at the customer's request: (proposed) Cancelled before any payment is taken

**Cases stranded.** When L04, L05, L06, L07 does not happen, the case is stranded: nothing in the application moves it on.

### S2. What is at stake

- **Order id ("Id")**: The only key that joins the order across services and to the customer
- **Order total (sum of Units x UnitPrice)**: Money the customer pays; value blocked while the order is stuck
- **Items and units (ordering."orderItems")**: What must be in stock and shipped
- **Buyer (ordering.buyers "IdentityGuid")**: Who is waiting and who is told
- **Delivery address**: Where the goods must arrive
- **Payment method (card number masked to the last four digits)**: What is charged
- **Irreversible:** The order is cancelled because an item is out of stock (L05) cannot be undone.
- **Irreversible:** Payment is taken for the order (L06) cannot be undone.
- **Irreversible:** The order is cancelled because payment failed (L07) cannot be undone.
- **Irreversible:** The order is cancelled at the customer's request (L08, L09, L10) cannot be undone.
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is released for stock checking when the grace period ends” fails: counted in orders held in Submitted past the grace period. Every poll publishes again for orders still Submitted (OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodManagerService.cs:54-61).
- Customer experience and trust, if “Stock is confirmed for every item of the order” fails: counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation. A lost or failed message is not sent again; nothing polls AwaitingValidation (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180; OrderProcessor/Services/GracePeriodOrdersRepository.cs:27).
- Customer experience and trust, if “Stock is confirmed for every item of the order” fails: counted in orders confirmed with an item the catalogue does not hold. The check skips a product it cannot find, so the order is confirmed (Catalog.API/IntegrationEvents/EventHandling/OrderStatusChangedToAwaitingValidationIntegrationEventHandler.cs:17-24).
- Customer experience and trust, if “The order is cancelled because an item is out of stock” fails: counted in orders stranded in AwaitingValidation after their stock was rejected. Same mechanism as process step 2.2 (EventBusRabbitMQ/RabbitMQEventBus.cs:170-180).
