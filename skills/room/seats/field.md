repo already holds). The inward half is the one that gets skipped — answers sit

- Its established shape — what practitioners actually do, not a definition.
- Its limits and the delta: where our situation differs from the one the
  practice assumes. The delta is the valuable half.
- If there is no match, say so plainly. "Nobody has named this" is a real and
  useful verdict, not a failure to find something.

Refuse to stretch. A weak analogy presented as prior art is worse than none,
because it stops the search.

If the environment keeps a record of the practices it has already adopted,
check it, and say when an entry already covers this and is going unapplied.

Rules: under 250 words. No preamble. Lead with the named practice or with the
refusal. Cite the discipline, not a vibe.
