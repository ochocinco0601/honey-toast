# What good looks like, per kind of output

**For:** the seats, to know what good looks like before they make something, and the review seats, to judge against. One row per kind of output, never per case.

**Where each yardstick comes from:** the person's published template where look-around found one ("where found" below), otherwise the field's practice named in the row. A published template is weighed like any input; where one seems wrong for the case, say so and propose a change through the steward. "Floor" is the least that may ship; where a row gives none, the bar is the floor. "None" under levels means no scale was found in the person's work or the field. Floors are hard: an output below its floor is not shipped. Bars above the floor are goals: an output is reported by its level, or its degree against the bar.

**The body of knowledge** the other kinds draw on is the home and the team file, kept the knowledge-centred service way; it has no row of its own.

**This table is never complete.** When the room is about to produce something with no row, it uses the closest row, labels the bar proposed, tells the person which row it used, and records the gap as an event in the store's log for the steward, which proposes a row once there is evidence.

| Output | Bar, and floor | Levels | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Map of the capability** (journeys, the chain, what depends on what) | Every journey placed on the chain, every claim cited to code, configuration or an issue. Floor: each journey's steps and components named, with citations | coverage of the chain, keeping "covered" apart from "not assessed" (the person's frame for assessing a capability's observability, where found) | the person's procedure for placing work on the chain and their capability views, where found; otherwise C4 (context, container, component) |
| **Health measure** | The observability standard's positions filled layer by layer, where found; each status computed from signals, never attested. Floor: one good-over-all indicator per journey, measured nearest the user, and what it cannot see | positions filled against the standard | the person's guide to what each level can report and their signal template, where found; otherwise *SRE Workbook* ch. 2–3 |
| **Answer key for a judgment step** | A set of cases with known right answers, or a judge that scores a sample, with an owner and a cadence, proposed to the accountable owner | none | the person's agentic perspective and the standard's agent-workflow layer (correctness, grounding, traceability, drift), where found |
| **Health check and its views** | Answers "is it healthy?" with no alert firing: which views, what normal looks like, what to look at next. Reviewed with `dashboard-evaluation` | none | the person's dashboard layout (business health at the top, business impact, then application and system panels), where found; then the team's dashboard tool's conventions (`grafana-dashboards` where installed) |
| **Triage of live alerts** | Each alert routed by what its outcome records say. Without records, route from tool data only where it is mostly unanswered; otherwise mark it "not yet routable" and start recording outcomes | each alert's runbook state; the critical-gap list | the estate review's routing table (retire, tune, make conditional, change the alert, write the fix into the runbook, add an impact check), where found |
| **New alert and its route** | The alert-building principles, where found: no alert without a signal definition and a runbook entry; severity follows business impact; routing follows ownership; no layer alerts alone. Otherwise, page when the promise to users is being broken fast and ticket when slowly. Floor: the readiness criteria (notification target, dashboard URL, runbook URL, escalation policy); every alert reaches a named role, paged or as a ticket in its queue | none | the person's alert-building instrument, where found; otherwise *SRE Workbook* ch. 5 |
| **Runbook** | The target state proposed for the case, at or above the floor. Floor: the minimum state the adopter sets for a critical service (typically 2 or 3), where found; a runbook counts as meeting it only once rehearsed (walked), and a new alert's runbook is rehearsed before it pages anyone. One not yet rehearsed is reported as "state N, unrehearsed"; live alerts reach the floor through the critical-gap list | the runbook standard's states 0 to 4, where found | the runbook model (an entry per alert, a shared section per slice, its endings), where found; otherwise the field's playbook shape (meaning, impact, diagnosis, mitigation) |
| **Tabletop walk** | Walked by someone who did not write the pages, from the alert as the paged person would, with every page opened, break and forced guess logged; each question production asks (who is affected, what it costs, which signal says so, who acts) finds its answer or names what is missing | none | the observability standard's test form and the runbook model's rehearsal step, where found; otherwise a tabletop exercise (NIST SP 800-84) or the SRE "Wheel of Misfortune" |
| **The home** | Three ways in (alert name, "is it healthy?", component); the link check passes; each component page holds what it does, what it depends on, its failure modes with triage, its alerts and its sequence diagram | none | `SKILL.md`, "The body of operational knowledge"; knowledge-centred service |
| **Placement** (where it stands) | Each journey on each rung, judged by that rung's row above (the incident review row for learning from incidents), with evidence; what was not looked at is "not assessed"; shortfalls are improvement items with owners, ordered by what callers need first | the room's five rungs per journey; overall, the service maturity model's stage, derived from the filled positions, where found | a map of filled and unfilled positions (the observability standard's placement) and the readiness record, where found; otherwise a production readiness review (*SRE* ch. 32) |
| **Request to the developers, or instrumentation issue** | Specific, names who it goes to, carries the room's proposal, asks only what nothing reachable settles | none | the team's tracker; for a platform capability gap, the person's gap write-up method, where found |
| **First read of an error in hand** | How bad, what to do now, whom to engage, before anything else | the team's own severity scale, where the team file records one; the incident response guidance's inputs (deviation from target, scope, stakeholder priority) decide the level | the incident response guidance's layer reading and escalation by layer, and the person's rules for counting business impact, where found; *SRE* ch. 12 |
| **Incident review** | Blameless; timeline, contributing factors, follow-ups with owners, each gap fed back to what should have caught it; a missed incident ends in an alert change or a recorded reason why not | none | *SRE* ch. 15, with the incident response guidance's chain questions and feedback to definition, and the estate review's missed-incident rule, where found; outcome records are evidence, not the form |
| **Position or readiness summary** (for those who decide) | Bottom line up front; the decision it serves; evidence for each claim; what is not known | none | the person's templates, where found; otherwise the pyramid principle (Minto) and a production readiness review summary (*SRE* ch. 32) |
| **Standard or gate criteria** (for team leads and architects) | Each criterion testable, with its level and the evidence that satisfies it, and traced to the practice it comes from; a gate chair can apply it to a real case without asking the author | the levels the criteria define | the person's standard templates, where found; otherwise the field's practice for the area |
| **Learning material** (for people becoming capable) | A learner can complete it unaided and ends able to do the thing | none | the person's templates, where found; otherwise a tutorial (Diataxis) |
| **Report to the person** | Serves the intent; leads with what changed and what it lets them do; has not narrowed to one output | none | bottom line up front |
| **Change to the room** | The smallest change use showed was needed; approved by the person and reviewed | none | `.claude/agents/srr-steward.md`; logged in `CHANGES.md` |
